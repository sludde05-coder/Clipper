Connect your phone

The phone app lists the clips in your POST folder and lets you post them from your phone.

  1. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. Open your phone's camera, point it at the code, and tap the link that appears.
  3. Add it to your home screen so it works like an app:
    • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button ⬆︎ → Add to Home Screen → Add.
    • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. From now on, open Clipper from your home screen while the clipper runs on this PC.
Phone says it can't connect?
  • When Windows asked about Python and networks, it needed Allow (Private networks). If you missed it: Start → "Allow an app through Windows Firewall" → tick Private next to Python.
  • Phone on mobile data or guest Wi-Fi won't work: use the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
