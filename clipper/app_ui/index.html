Posting

Link the platforms you post to. When you press Post, Clipper goes through each one and fills in the upload page for you in its own Chrome window, so you only check it and click Post.

Sign in once
Opens a normal Chrome window with Clipper's own profile. Sign in to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram there (Google sign-in works in this window), then close it. Clipper then uses those logins when it fills in the upload pages.
Fill in the upload page for me
Clipper puts the video and caption in, in its own Google Chrome window (profile "Clipper"), and you click Post. The sites can see it's an automated browser, so if one starts asking for extra checks, turn this off: then Clipper opens the page, shows the file and copies the caption instead.
Also click Post for me
Clipper waits for each upload, clicks Post / Publish / Share, checks the site confirms it, and moves on to the next clip by itself. TikTok, YouTube and Instagram forbid posting with automated browsers, so this carries a real risk of the account being limited or banned. Needs "Fill in the upload page for me" on.
Minutes between clips
to minutes
Each wait is a random time in this range (e.g. 8 to 10), so posts don't come at a fixed rhythm. Posting many clips back-to-back looks like a bot.
